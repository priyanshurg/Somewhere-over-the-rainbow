Renewable Electricity Use

What it measures:
Share of farm electricity sourced from renewable energy.

How to measure:

Energy bills

Solar/wind generation data

Renewable contracts (RECs/GOs)

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Electricity from fossil fuels (grid mix) has significant CO₂ emissions.
Switching to renewables can lower the footprint of milk production.

How to increase renewable use:

Install solar on barns

Use energy-efficient motors and equipment

Sign renewable purchasing agreements
