# Cowerful Mitigation Guide

This guide outlines practical, realistic methods dairy farmers can use to reduce carbon footprint.

---

## 1. Improve Feed Efficiency
- Increase forage quality (cut earlier, improve silage fermentation)
- Reduce feed sorting
- Optimize ration balancing
- Provide consistent cow comfort (lying time, heat stress reduction)

---

## 2. Reduce Fertilizer Use
- Use soil testing and precision application
- Incorporate clover/legume pastures
- Apply manure when cool and dry to minimize losses
- Avoid overapplication in autumn/wet seasons

---

## 3. Increase Protein Efficiency
- Match protein supply to animal needs
- Reduce crude protein % in diet where feasible
- Adjust rations to reduce nitrogen excretion

---

## 4. Improve Manure Management
- Adopt biogas systems or slurry acidification
- Maintain a crust on slurry tanks
- Reduce storage time before spreading
- Use low-emission spreading equipment

---

## 5. Replace Soy with Low-Carbon Alternatives
- Rapeseed meal
- Sunflower meal
- Brewer’s grains
- Local forage expansion

---

## 6. Increase Renewable Electricity Use
- Solar panels  
- Wind energy contracts  
- Farm-scale biogas CHP  

---

## 7. Reduce Animal Mortality and Replacement Rate
- Improve cow comfort
- Better hoof care
- Reduced mastitis through hygiene and bedding management
- Regular health scoring

---

## 8. Increase Grazing (Where System Allows)
- Rotational grazing increases forage quality
- Reduces imported feed emissions
- Improves soil carbon sequestration
