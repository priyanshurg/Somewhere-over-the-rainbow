Land Use

What it measures:
Total land area required to produce feed, grazing, and farm operations per liter of FPCM.

How to measure:

Sum all owned or rented cropland and pasture (ha), divided by volume of FPCM produced.

Why it influences carbon footprint:
More land used → higher emissions from tillage, fertilizer, machinery, and potentially land conversion.

What drives high land use:

Low crop yields

Inefficient grazing

Overreliance on purchased forages

Poor soil health

How to reduce land-related emissions:

Improve forage yield and quality

Use legumes to reduce fertilizer needs

Adopt precision agriculture

Increase stocking efficiency on pasture
