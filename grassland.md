Permanent Grassland

What it measures:
Long-term grassland (>5 years) not regularly ploughed.

How to measure:

Farm maps and land registry data

Pasture records

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Permanent grasslands store large amounts of carbon in soil and protect against erosion.
They can lower the farm’s net emissions.

How to increase carbon benefits:

Avoid ploughing

Use diverse grass/legume mixes

Implement rotational grazing
