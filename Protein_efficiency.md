Protein Efficiency

What it measures:
How effectively cows convert dietary protein into milk protein.

How to measure:

Milk protein output (kg) from milk testing

Total dietary protein consumed

Calculation:
Protein efficiency = milk protein produced ÷ total protein intake.

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Unused nitrogen from protein becomes manure N → leads to nitrous oxide (N₂O) emissions, a highly potent greenhouse gas.
Higher protein efficiency → less nitrogen loss → lower footprint.

What affects it:

Balance of amino acids

Crude protein levels

Forage quality

Rumen health

How farmers can improve:

Reduce excess crude protein

Use amino acid balancing

Improve forage digestibility

Monitor rumen pH and cow health
