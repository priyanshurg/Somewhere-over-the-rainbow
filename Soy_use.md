Soy Use

What it measures:
Amount of soymeal or soy-based products in feed rations.

How to measure:

Feed purchase logs

Ration formulation software

Typically expressed in kg per cow per year

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Soy often carries high upstream emissions, especially if linked to deforestation or long transport chains.

What affects soy use:

Diet protein requirements

Price of soymeal

Availability of alternative protein feeds

How to optimize soy use:

Replace part of soy with local protein feeds

Improve forage quality to reduce protein supplement needs

Use canola meal, DDGS, or lupin where feasible
