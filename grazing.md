Grazing

What it measures:
Share of cow diet from fresh pasture.

How to measure:

Estimate dry matter intake (DMI) from grazing

% of total feed intake

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Grazing reduces emissions from feed harvesting, transport, and machinery.
But poor grazing can lead to methane inefficiencies.

How to improve grazing efficiency:

Rotational grazing systems

Maintain pasture height and rest periods

Add legumes to improve forage quality
