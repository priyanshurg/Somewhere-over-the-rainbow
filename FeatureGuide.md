# Cowerful Feature Guide
Below is a farmer-friendly explanation of every feature used in the Cowerful model. These definitions come from the Arla Foods decarbonization program.

---

## Feed_inefficiency
**Definition:**  
kg of total feed (dry matter) needed to produce 1 kg of fat- and protein-corrected milk (FPCM).

**Interpretation:**  
Lower values = more efficient.  
High feed inefficiency means cows need more feed to produce milk → more emissions.

**Typical range:**  
0.7 – 1.6  
- 50th percentile: 0.99  
- 75th percentile: 1.11

---

## Protein_efficiency
**Definition:**  
Share of nitrogen intake that becomes milk, meat, or animal growth.

**Interpretation:**  
Higher values = better nitrogen use efficiency, meaning less nitrogen is lost as emissions.

**Typical range:**  
17.7 – 38.5  
- Median: 29  
- High values (>32) are top performers.

---

## Animal_mortality
**Definition:**  
% of cows that died on the farm in a given year.

**Interpretation:**  
Higher mortality increases emissions because replacement animals must be raised.

**Typical range:**  
0 – 15.5%  
- Median: 4.33%

---

## Fertilizer_use
**Definition:**  
kg of nitrogen applied per hectare from manure + artificial fertilizer.

**Interpretation:**  
Higher N increases nitrous oxide emissions. Precision application lowers footprint.

**Typical range:**  
45 – 490 kg/ha  
- Median: 206  
- 75th percentile: 274

---

## Land_use
**Definition:**  
Square meters of land required per kg milk to grow all feed.

**Interpretation:**  
Higher land use often means reliance on low-yield land or extensive systems.

**Typical range:**  
0.6 – 3.6 m²/kg  
- Median: 1.31

---

## Soy_use
**Definition:**  
Grams of soy used per kg milk.

**Interpretation:**  
Soy has higher upstream emissions due to land use change.

**Typical range:**  
0 – 86 g/kg  
- Median: 3.43  
- 75th percentile: 14.4

---

## Permanent_grassland
**Definition:**  
Hectares per cow of permanent/natural grassland.

**Interpretation:**  
More permanent grassland can improve biodiversity but may increase land use emissions.

**Typical range:**  
0.1 – 3.0  
- Median: 0.77

---

## Grazing
**Definition:**  
% of cows that graze at least 6 hours/day for 120+ days using rotational grazing.

**Interpretation:**  
More grazing can reduce feed emissions and increase carbon sequestration.

**Typical range:**  
0 – 73%  
- Median: 16%

---

## Manure_mgmt
**Definition:**  
1 = farm uses acidification or digests ≥75% of manure for biogas  
0 = otherwise

**Interpretation:**  
Manure biogas systems significantly lower methane emissions.

**Distribution:**  
Median: 0  
75th percentile: 1

---

## Renewable_electricity_use
**Definition:**  
1 = electricity is 100% renewable  
0 = not fully renewable

**Interpretation:**  
Switching to renewable electricity reduces CO₂ emissions.

**Distribution:**  
Median: 0  
75th percentile: 1

---

## Carbon_footprint
**Definition:**  
kg CO₂e emitted per kg milk produced.

**Typical range:**  
0.74 – 2.08  
- Median: 1.11
