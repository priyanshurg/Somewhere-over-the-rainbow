Fertilizer Use

What it measures:
Synthetic nitrogen fertilizer applied to crops and forages.

How to measure:

Track fertilizer purchases and application rates

Express in kg nitrogen per hectare (kg N/ha)

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Manufacturing N fertilizer is energy-intensive.
Applying N releases N₂O, one of the most potent greenhouse gases.

What increases emissions:

Overapplication

Poor timing

No soil testing

Low manure recycling

How to reduce emissions:

Use soil testing to match crop needs

Split applications to reduce losses

Use manure as fertilizer

Apply nitrification inhibitors when appropriate
