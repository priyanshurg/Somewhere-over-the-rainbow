Feed Inefficiency

What it measures:
Feed inefficiency shows how much feed is consumed beyond what is needed for the milk and growth output. Higher values mean more feed input for the same production.

How to measure:

Track daily feed intake (kg dry matter).

Record milk yield or weight gain.

Calculate:
Feed inefficiency = Actual feed intake – expected feed intake for a given production level.

Farmers often rely on feed efficiency or feed conversion ratio calculators.

Why it influences carbon footprint:
Feed production (fertilizer, transport, land use) is one of the highest emission sources on dairy farms.
Higher inefficiency → more feed grown/transported → more emissions.

What increases it:

Poor ration formulation

Spoiled or wasted feed

Illness or heat stress

Overfeeding

Inaccurate mixing or TMR variation

How farmers can improve it:

Balance rations with a nutritionist

Improve forage quality

Reduce feed spoilage and sorting

Monitor cow health and stress

Adopt precision feeding tools
